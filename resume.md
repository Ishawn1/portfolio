## Featured Engineering Projects

### SmartKheti — Autonomous Edge-AI Precision Agriculture System
*Flutter, Dart, C++, TensorFlow Lite, INT8 YOLO26, ESP32-S3, RS485 Modbus* | [GitHub](https://github.com/Ishawn1/Latent-Protocol)
- Architected a zero-cloud precision agriculture ecosystem combining an ESP32-S3 microcontroller field unit with an offline Android Flutter companion app engineered for rural low-connectivity drylands.
- Implemented a hand-crafted 3-layer neural network (6→16→8→2) in native C++ executing inside ESP32 SRAM, achieving **0.02 ms on-chip inference latency** for live soil stress and anomaly risk detection.
